Request account deletion

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This page explains how users of Trainer OS can request deletion of their account and associated data.

Steps to request deletion

  1. Email alkesh26@gmail.com from the phone number or email associated with your account (or clearly state that phone number in the message).
  2. Use the subject line: Trainer OS account deletion request.
  3. Include: your full name (as in the app), your sign-in phone number with country code (for example +91…), and whether you are a trainer or client.
  4. We will verify ownership of the account and confirm when deletion is complete. We aim to process requests within 30 days.

Data that is deleted

  • Account / authentication record tied to your phone number
  • Profile details (name, gym, goals, weight, and similar fields you entered)
  • Workout plans, session logs, leave records, and payment records linked to your account
  • Client–trainer links and invite records associated with your account
  • Notification preferences stored for your account

Data that may be retained (limited)

  • Records we must keep temporarily for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance (if any), for up to 90 days, then permanently deleted where applicable
  • Aggregated or anonymized analytics that cannot reasonably identify you (if any)

Trainer accounts

If you are a trainer, deleting your account removes your trainer profile and associated coaching data under your control. Clients linked only to your account may lose access to that coaching workspace; those clients can separately request deletion of their own accounts.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: alkesh26@gmail.com
