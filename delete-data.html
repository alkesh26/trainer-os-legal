Request data deletion

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This page explains how users of Trainer OS can request deletion of some or all of their data without deleting their entire account.

To close your account completely, use the account deletion page instead.

Steps to request data deletion

  1. Email alkesh26@gmail.com with subject: Trainer OS data deletion request.
  2. Include your full name, sign-in phone number with country code (for example +91…), and whether you are a trainer or client.
  3. Clearly list the data you want deleted (for example: workout history, payment records, leave history, profile weight/goals, or “all optional data but keep my account”).
  4. We will verify ownership and process the request. We aim to complete it within 30 days.

Data you can request to delete

  • Profile details you entered (name display fields, gym, goals, weight)
  • Workout plans and session logs
  • Leave / absence records
  • Payment / session-pack records linked to your profile
  • Client notes and similar coaching notes associated with you

Data that may be kept

  • Core account identifiers needed to keep your account working (for example phone number used for sign-in), unless you also request full account deletion
  • Records retained temporarily for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance (if any), for up to 90 days, then deleted where applicable
  • Aggregated or anonymized information that cannot reasonably identify you (if any)

Contact

Data deletion requests: alkesh26@gmail.com
