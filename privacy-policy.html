Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Trainer OS (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a coaching workspace for personal trainers and their clients. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Phone number for sign-in and account identification
  • Profile details you enter (name, gym, goals, weight)
  • Workout plans, session logs, payments, and leave records
  • Device notification preferences stored on your phone

How we use it

  • Run your trainer–client coaching workspace
  • Show schedules, reminders, and progress in the app
  • Secure access so trainers see only their clients and vice versa

Storage & security

  • Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication)
  • Access is enforced with role-based Firestore security rules
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties

Your choices

  • Update profile fields available in the app at any time
  • Turn off push reminders in Profile → Notifications
  • Contact support to request account or data deletion

Data retention & deletion

We retain account and coaching data while your account is active so the app can function. To request deletion of your account or associated data, email alkesh26@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Children’s privacy

Trainer OS is intended for adults using the app for fitness coaching. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party services

We use Google Firebase for authentication and database storage. Their processing of data is governed by Google’s privacy policies in addition to this policy.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions or requests: alkesh26@gmail.com

To delete your account and data, see our Account deletion page.
